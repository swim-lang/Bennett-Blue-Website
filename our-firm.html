A few things
worth knowing

Do I need to live in Montana?

Not necessarily. A Montana entity may own and register a vehicle, but your circumstances and the requirements where the vehicle is kept and used must be considered. We can help you evaluate whether the arrangement is appropriate.

Can you help before I buy?

Yes. An early conversation allows us to consider the ownership structure and documentation before your purchase is complete.

What if the vehicle is financed?

Lender requirements matter. Ownership through an LLC may require written consent and additional documentation, which should be addressed before proceeding.

Can you help after registration is complete?

Yes. Our services include registration renewals, ongoing LLC support, and assistance when your ownership arrangements change.
